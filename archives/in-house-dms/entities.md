# Entities

The things actors act on.

1. Order
2. Shipment (carries the AWB)
3. Barcode (Shipments and Consignments can carry different barcodes)
4. Sorting Bin
5. Manifest
6. Consignment
7. Serving Location
8. Serviced Location
9. Vehicle
10. Device
11. People
12. Money
13. Ticket
14. Vendor

Serving Location is where an order is served from, and Serviced Location is where it is served to. In Forward, these are warehouses and DCs, and the Customer's location. In Reverse, they flip.
