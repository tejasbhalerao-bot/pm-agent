# Open questions

1. **Reassigning shipments.** The Dispatcher job "Reassign shipments quickly when a driver can't complete their batch, a delivery fails, or an order is cancelled or changed after dispatch" is to be revisited when actions are drafted.
2. **Where the system starts.** The System generates the AWB, yet WH Ops and the Dispatcher jobs begin at "ready for dispatch", which follows AWB print. The step that starts the system's involvement needs to be stated.
3. **Control Tower.** It has one job: watch live deliveries and step in on problems. The Team Lead ("spot and fix delivery problems in the city") and Central Logistics Ops ("step in where performance drops") overlap with it. The split between the three needs defining.
4. **Vendor payment basis.** Finance pays vendors "under the pay rules", and Central Logistics Ops defines pay, incentives and penalties. The basis of payment (for example per trip, per consignment or per delivery), and whether the rules apply to vendors or to drivers, is not defined.
